# Judging criteria and evidence

Source: Participant Guidebook, “Judging Evaluation Criteria and Standardized Scoring Rubric.” This is the team's planning summary, not an official score or an eligibility decision.

## Standard rubric: 40 points

Four criteria, ten points each. Levels: 0–3 Developing, 4–6 Proficient, 7–8 Advanced, 9–10 Exceptional.

| Criterion | Maximum | Internal owner |

The guide requires both AI/ML labs before solution development. Actual completion/order is recorded separately; a good technical score would not establish eligibility.

## 1. Community value and theme

Judges assess the problem, beneficiaries and how the chosen theme appears in the implemented solution.

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Strong impact on a critical need; theme integrated throughout |
| 7–8 | Clear real-world problem, benefits and theme fit |
| 4–6 | Need addressed, but benefits or theme connection remain vague |

Identify a concrete beneficiary, support the problem with sourced data and show why the design fits **AI Secured**. The Mexican figures describe distinct populations/events; they are not a linked care-loss cohort. Explain the trusted-admin demo boundary and use synthetic profiles. Align with **AI for Good** without claiming demonstrated clinical benefit.

## 2. Novelty and originality

The rubric emphasizes new ideas and an event-built original solution.

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Highly original, advanced and implemented end to end |
| 7–8 | Distinctive innovative concept |
| 4–6 | Creative integration with a common or predictable approach |

Defend the integration and measurable privacy properties, while acknowledging that Paillier and logistic regression are established techniques. Preserve frequent, factual commits and prepared-data disclosures. Show a working data → model → encrypted service → hospital flow rather than a disconnected notebook.

## 3. Technical design and LinuxONE

Judges expect AI/ML actually running in LinuxONE Community Cloud, open-source packages and screenshots of platform capabilities used.

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Advanced architecture and strong demonstrated platform use |
| 7–8 | Solid architecture with substantial LinuxONE AI/ML execution |
| 4–6 | Basic platform use or limited AI/ML evidence |
| 0–3 | Minimal or absent demonstrated LinuxONE use |

Train and infer on the real VM. Show containers, architecture, model, resource/timing evidence and key separation. Use the actual protocol tests. `s390x` alone does not prove the cloud provider. Do not attribute CPACF acceleration or Secure Execution without supporting evidence. The optional predictive/generative or CKKS work must not delay required evidence. An architecture flowchart helps communicate the design.

## 4. Execution and pitch

The required video covers problem, supporting data, solution and IBM Z usage, including stack, instance and model on screen.

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Clear, polished pitch with a convincing working demo |
| 7–8 | Strong communication and solid execution |
| 4–6 | Working project with incomplete or weak communication |

Use the [English script](../submission/PITCH.md), rehearse transitions and target 170 seconds, leaving margin under the 180-second limit. Record clear authorized narration and actual LinuxONE footage. The silent editing draft is not the final video. Check public links without a login and export the required PDF.

## AI Innovation Excellence: separate 20-point rubric

| Criterion | Maximum | Highest-level evidence |
|-----------|--------:|------------------------|
| AI innovation and originality | 10 | A distinctive, unconventional AI approach supported by the implementation |
| Transformative impact and technical excellence | 10 | A sophisticated end-to-end system with demonstrated LinuxONE use and credible impact |

This is a separate award rubric, not necessarily the regional first-place result. Originality and measured platform execution matter more than adding an unverified model name.

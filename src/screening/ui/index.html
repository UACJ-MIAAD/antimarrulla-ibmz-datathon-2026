Privacy-preserving screening

Connecting…SYNTHETIC DATA

Answers use the codes of the CDC Diabetes Health Indicators dataset (UCI 891, BRFSS 2015). These are recoded dataset values, not raw BRFSS response codes. Every field is required. Do not enter real patient data.

A risk model scores a patient for diabetes without ever seeing their answers.

  1. 01
    HOSPITAL

    Encrypts the 21 answers

    Each answer is encrypted with the hospital's 2048-bit Paillier public key. The private key never leaves the hospital.

  2. 02
    RISK SERVICE

    Scores them while encrypted

    Over mutual TLS 1.3, it computes the encrypted score Enc(z) from the ciphertexts. It holds no private key and cannot read anything.

  3. 03
    HOSPITAL

    Decrypts only the score

    It turns the score into a probability, decides whether to prioritize a glucose test and stores the result in an AES-256-GCM vault.
